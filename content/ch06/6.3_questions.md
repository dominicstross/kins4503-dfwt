### Applied Short Answer (sa)

**Clinical/Athletic Prompt:** A triathlete increases their weekly running and cycling volume by 50% over 4 weeks while reducing caloric intake. They report persistent fatigue, muscle soreness, and a decline in race times. Evaluate their training program from a body composition and metabolic balance perspective using textbook principles.

**Model Answer:** The triathlete's drastic increase in training volume paired with caloric restriction has likely triggered a predominance of catabolic activity in the body, creating a severe imbalance between catabolic and anabolic processes. While appropriate aerobic training with proper nutrition reduces relative body fat while preserving fat-free mass, excessive volume combined with inadequate energy intake accelerates protein degradation and impairs tissue repair. This catabolic state leads to persistent fatigue, loss of recovery capacity, and performance decrements characteristic of nonfunctional overreaching. The athlete must reduce training volume, optimize nutritional intake, and restore anabolic-catabolic balance.

**Evaluation Criteria:**
- Identifies the state of catabolic-anabolic imbalance caused by excessive volume and restricted nutrition.
- Explains that excessive aerobic volume drives catabolic breakdown over tissue recovery.
- Recommends adjusting volume and nutrition to restore physiological balance and performance.

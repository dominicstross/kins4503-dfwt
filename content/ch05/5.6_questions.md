Additionally, prior contractile activity causes peripheral neuromuscular fatigue, reducing motor unit firing frequencies and force capacity, which Leveritt & Abernethy showed reduces squat repetitions by 13% to 36%. The practical programming rule is to perform high-priority resistance training first in the workout session (or separate sessions by several hours) when explosive power and maximal strength are the primary training goals.
